The judgment also requires Meta to make changes for underage users. ## [Postgres 300x Faster for Analytics: pgrust's Batching, Fusion, and SIMD](https://malisper.me/how-we-made-postgres-hundreds-of-times-faster-the-query-engine/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The pgrust project, a Rust re-implementation of Postgres, demonstrates how batching, operator fusion, and SIMD can make analytics queries hundreds of times faster. The post details these techniques and their impact on query execution. These gains could bring vectorized, analytical performance to the Postgres ecosystem without breaking wire or SQL compatibility. If adopted, it may reduce the need for separate analytics engines and expand Postgres's role in data-intensive workloads. pgrust is wire compatible and SQL dialect compatible with Postgres, and can even run in the browser via WebAssembly. The author reports formal verification and differential fuzz testing have proven over 1,000 user-facing functions match Postgres's logic.

hackernews · poly2it · Aug 7, 11:00 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49208535)

**Background**: Traditional Postgres is row-oriented, which is efficient for OLTP but slow for analytical scans. Batching processes rows in chunks, operator fusion merges adjacent pipeline operators to reduce overhead, and SIMD lets a CPU apply one instruction to many data points simultaneously, enabling faster analytics. pgrust is an experimental rewrite that explores these optimizations while preserving Postgres compatibility.

## [AMD acquires Taalas to etch AI models into silicon for faster inference](https://www.theregister.com/systems/2026/08/06/amd-acquires-ai-chip-startup-taalas-to-boost-inference-performance-by-etching-models-into-silicon/5284344) ⭐️ 8.0/10

AMD announced a definitive agreement to acquire Taalas, an AI inference chip startup that hardwires specific AI models directly into silicon. Taalas' current chip runs a small version of Meta's Llama 3.1, and the team, led by co-founder and former Tenstorrent CEO Ljubisa Bajic, will join AMD. This acquisition signals that dedicated inference silicon is becoming a competitive necessity, as both AMD and Nvidia are betting that frontier model weights will run on fixed silicon rather than shared GPU pools. It could reshape AI inference economics by offering dramatically lower power and cost for model serving, and intensify competition with Nvidia. AMD plans to integrate Taalas' technology for system-level solutions together with its Instinct GPUs and CPUs. Taalas' technology optimizes AI inference dataflows, reducing compute and memory bottlenecks associated with general-purpose architectures; the startup is working on chips for larger, more advanced models.

hackernews · itvision · Aug 6, 20:23 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49201970)

**Background**: AI inference is the process by which a trained model generates responses to user input, and it traditionally runs on general-purpose GPUs. Instead of executing model weights in software on flexible hardware, Taalas' approach 'etches' model weights into custom silicon so the model runs as fixed hardware, which can be faster and far more power-efficient. This is similar to how video decoding became a nearly free, on-chip capability after being handled by CPUs.

## [US Reviews Chinese AI Firms' Offshore Access to Nvidia Chips](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/us-reviews-china-s-offshore-access-to-nvidia-chips-after-ai-breakthroughs) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The U.S. Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) is systematically reviewing how Chinese AI companies obtain and use Nvidia chips abroad, including via remote access to computing power rented in other countries. The review, which includes compiling lists of black-market locations and countries where Chinese firms rent chips, was launched after Moonshot AI released its Kimi K3 model, which a White House official accused of being built using illegally obtained Nvidia chips accessed remotely through Thailand. This review could fundamentally reshape the effectiveness of U.S. export controls, because remote cloud access may allow Chinese firms to use advanced chips without physically importing them. It raises legal questions about BIS's authority over cloud computing agreements and could push forward new legislation that Nvidia and other tech companies oppose, affecting the global AI and semiconductor industries. The review includes compiling two lists: locations of black markets suspected of smuggling restricted chips into China, and countries where Chinese companies remotely rent chips. Because remote access itself is not illegal, BIS's authority to restrict such cloud computing agreements is uncertain; the U.S. House has passed a bipartisan bill to explicitly grant that authority, but it faces opposition from Nvidia. The report also says Alibaba, via a Singapore shell company controlled by a Cayman entity, used Nvidia chips in Malaysia through Megaspeed, which is under U.S. investigation.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 7, 11:18

**Background**: BIS is an agency of the U.S. Department of Commerce that deals with issues involving national security and high technology, and it enforces export controls on advanced semiconductors. Historically, U.S. export controls have focused on physical shipments of chips, but cloud-based access is a gray area because renting computing power overseas does not involve moving hardware across borders. The Remote Access Security Act (H.R. 2683), passed by the House in January 2026, would explicitly extend export controls to remote access, but it has not become law and faces industry opposition.

## [sub2api OAuth flaw allows full account takeover with only victim's email](https://github.com/Wei-Shaw/sub2api/issues/5350) ⭐️ 8.0/10

sub2api versions v0.1.171 and earlier contain a high-severity OAuth account takeover vulnerability rated CVSS 8.8. An attacker who only knows the victim's registered email can bind their own OAuth identity to the victim's account without any password, verification code, or user interaction. This is critical because successful exploitation gives an attacker full control over the victim's API keys, billing balance, and subscription quotas in the sub2api gateway. Given sub2api's role as an AI API proxy, this flaw could lead to unauthorized usage, financial loss, and disruption for many self-hosted users. The flaw lies in the pending session flow, where the existingUser branch fails to verify a password or verification code. An attacker can set the target user ID to the victim, complete the OAuth identity binding, and subsequently every OAuth login will resolve to the victim's account.

telegram · zaihuapd · Aug 7, 14:59

**Background**: Sub2API is an open-source AI API proxy that unifies subscriptions for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, and Antigravity, and is hosted on GitHub at Wei-Shaw/sub2api. OAuth is an open authorization standard that allows users to grant third-party applications access to their information without sharing passwords. In this context, a vulnerability in the OAuth identity binding process can directly compromise the entire account because the account is linked to an external identity provider.
